# Community guidelines

Hours welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, beliefs, and levels of experience. Participation does not require a particular religious affiliation.

- Treat other people with respect. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Keep discussions relevant to the app. Discuss liturgical questions with sources and patience.
- Do not harass people, publish their private information, or post discriminatory or sexually explicit material.
- Assume good intent, accept corrections, and give others room to learn.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Repeated or severe abuse may result in a ban.
