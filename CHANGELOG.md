# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial public release.

- Native Rust reader for the modern Roman Catholic Liturgy of the Hours in English.
- Five daily offices, date selection, section navigation, and local search.
- Explicit date downloads with offline reading and visible library coverage.
- Notes, bookmarks, highlights, saved reading positions, and prayer progress.
- Light, parchment, and dark themes with bundled Cinzel and Literata fonts.
- Library backup import and export.
- macOS app for Apple Silicon, with third-party notices and checksums.

Offline use requires downloading the desired dates first. Prayer texts are not bundled. The Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Other platforms are not yet verified.
